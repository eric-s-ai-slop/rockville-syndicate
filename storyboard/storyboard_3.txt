DESIGN & STORYBOARD EXTRACTION: PART 4
18. ALIGNMENT, INTELLIGENCE, & WARDROBE SYSTEMS
Character stats are fluid and dictate how NPCs and party members interact with the player.
The Intelligence Check (The 123Test):
Instead of a standard INT stat, characters must take a sketchy online IQ test.
Lore Result: Nick F scores an 85. Nick H scores an 83. They rationalize this as "basically a B" to avoid taking ego damage. Players with an 85+ IQ get unique, highly-delusional dialogue options.
The Alignment Grid (The Political Compass):
Determines faction loyalty. Eric uses the Political Compass test to label Nick H a "Libtard," permanently shifting Nick H's alignment and opening him up to "Joe Biden" gaslighting attacks during the Spotify Wars.
The Wardrobe / Armor System:
Dress Code "URBAN": Nick F's required attire for "Looney Tuesdays." Grants +10 Stealth in College Park but requires spending gold.
Dress Code "COUNTRY FORMAL": Jacob's preferred armor class. Wearing this to a city nightclub triggers the "City Boy, City Boy!" taunt from NPCs, dealing continuous psychic damage.
The "Fck It We Ball" Fit:* A legendary armor set Nick F equips when initiating a high-risk WTM. Grants immunity to logic.
19. ADVANCED TACTICAL COMBAT & THE "GAMEPIGEON" META
Combat in this game is entirely text-based and psychological. GamePigeon is the ultimate weaponized spell-casting system.
The 20 Questions Interrogation:
Spell Type: Reveal / Debuff.
Usage: Eric and Nick H use this to systematically dismantle Jacob's delusions. By forcing him to answer "Yes" or "No" (e.g., "Is she chopped?", "Are you delusional?"), they lock him into logical traps that drain his HP.
The Crazy 8s Flashbang:
Spell Type: Stun / Interrupt.
Usage: Cast exclusively at 1:58 AM or mid-argument. It completely derails the current conversation thread, resetting enemy aggro. If the opponent declines the game, they take "Cowardice" damage.
The Cup Pong Duel:
Spell Type: 1v1 Gladiator Match.
Lore: Used to settle unbreakable stalemates (e.g., Jacob vs. Nick H at 12:00 AM). The loser must concede the argument.
The "Clash Royale X-Bow" Ultimate:
Nick F's toxic meta-strategy. He plays Clash Royale from dawn to dusk using the X-Bow deck, a universally hated strategy that inflicts Enrage on Eric.
20. LATE-NIGHT EXPEDITIONS & THE "LIMINAL" QUEST BOARD
When the clock hits 10:30 PM, standard quests vanish, replaced by highly dangerous "Liminal" quests.
The Fake ID Vendor Quest:
Jacob acquires a Fake ID but suffers from the Scared debuff, preventing him from using it at the liquor store.
The "Talbots" Exploit: Nick H advises Jacob to use the ID at Talbots (a women's clothing store) because "they never card." This is a trap dialogue option that will result in maximum embarrassment.
Liminal Space Spelunking:
Eric and Nick F are obsessed with exploring "Liminal Spaces." Quest locations include Monocacy National Battlefield, Sugarloaf Mountain, and Dickerson MD.
Condition: These quests only spawn between 11:00 PM and 2:00 AM. Accepting them causes Nick H to immediately abandon the party to go to sleep.
The Tunnel Run:
A stealth/smoke mission. The party must sneak into "The Tunnel" to hit the "J". If Jacob is in the party, the mission fails because he skips class but refuses to inhale.
21. BASE CAMP MECHANICS: DIET, CONSUMABLES & HAZARDS
Managing the party's food and consumable intake is critical for surviving the grueling 2026 operational cycle.
The Grocery Raid Ban:
Lore: Nick F is permanently banned from solo grocery shopping.
Reason: During the previous Cabin Trip, he purchased 3 lbs of blueberry pancakes, raw salmon, and $300 worth of S'mores, resulting in Maharko starving and getting food poisoning. If Nick F is put in charge of provisions, the party loses all their gold and gains the Nauseous status effect.
Vendor Tier List (The Food War):
Honeypig ($32): High morale boost, high cost. Requires 2 days of logistical planning.
Hot Lola's ($10): Nick F's preferred vendor. Nick H considers it "mid."
McDonald's: Jacob's desperate bribe. He will offer to buy everyone McDonald's just to force a hangout.
Consumables:
Accutane: Cures the "Acne" debuff but makes the user intensely fatigued and hypersensitive to the sun. Nick F uses this as an excuse to avoid daytime events.
G-Leaf / 10mg Eddie: Restores sanity but massively increases the "Reaction Lag" stat, making the party fail time-sensitive quick-time events.
Cigarettes (Cig Summer): Jacob buys these solely to look "cool" (Aura +1, Health -5). Nick H heavily disapproves.
22. "LOST MEDIA" & COLLECTIBLES
Throughout the game, players can scour the chat logs to find "Lost Media" artifacts to blackmail party members.
Item 1: The "Rapist Stew" Sticker: An AI-generated piece of slop created by Nick F to permanently damage Maharko's reputation. Can be equipped to deal psychic damage in group chats.
Item 2: The "Heated Rivalry" Tape: A mysterious, heavily referenced movie/book the group constantly plans "watch parties" for. (Used as a lure to get Jacob to drive to College Park).
Item 3: The "Sub-Zero Mask": A cosmetic item Jacob equips when entering his "I don't care about girls anymore" persona.
Item 4: The 12-Year-Old Discord Log: A hazardous artifact. Jacob accidentally rizzes a 12-year-old on Discord. If Eric acquires this item, he gains permanent blackmail leverage over Jacob, threatening to send it to the authorities.
23. FACTION DYNAMICS & NPC EXPLOITS
The "Chris Rivas" Phantom Summon: Chris Rivas is an off-screen NPC. Jacob uses him as a fake alibi ("I'm hanging out with Chris Rivas tonight") to make the group jealous. The group can roll a Perception check to realize Jacob is actually sitting alone at Long John Silver's.
The Washington Commanders Rage-Bait:
Target: Nick Hedgecock.
Mechanic: Nick H is fiercely loyal to the Washington Commanders. If Jacob or Eric bring up a Commanders loss in the chat, Nick H enters a Blind Rage, threatens to reveal Jacob's secrets to his crush, and temporarily leaves the party.
The "Agent Buyback" Exploit:
Lore: When Nick F cancels the Cabin to fly to Spain, he attempts to keep the refund money.
Quest: The "Boca Syndicate" (Eric and Alex) must orchestrate an "Agent Buyback," threatening mutiny until Nick F processes the refunds via Robinhood.
