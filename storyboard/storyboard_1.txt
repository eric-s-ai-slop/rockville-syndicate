1. CORE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS
These are the foundational systems the AI should implement for the overworld and combat.
* The B.I.Q. (Battle IQ) System: Replaces traditional "Level/XP". BIQ dictates turn order, persuasion success, and resistance to gaslighting.
* The Time-of-Day Engine (The Deep Waters): The game world changes based on the in-game clock.
    * Pre-10 PM: Normal gameplay.
    * 10:00 PM (The Curse): Nick Hedgecock’s stats drop to 0.
    * 0100 - 0500 Hours (Deep Waters): Random encounters skyrocket. Nick Farrar gains infinite Stamina but loses cognitive reasoning. Jacob Lebby has a higher chance to trigger a "Systemic Melt."
* The WTM (What's The Move) Probe: The core quest-initiation system. A character drops "WTM" in the chat UI. The player must respond within a time limit (checking the Reaction Lag stat). Ignoring it causes Agility/Flake damage.
* Environmental Multipliers:
    * The Delusion Tax ( d M    dM ): A severe status debuff. Characters who believe in "Catfishes" or "Impossible Romances" take multiplicative damage to their logic stats.
    * Financial Leverage ( f i n M    finM ): Characters with administrative control over subscriptions (e.g., Spotify) can passively drain gold from other party members per turn.
* The GamePigeon Interruption System: In the middle of dialogue/combat, Eric or Nick F can deploy "Let's play CRAZY 8!" or "20 Questions" to completely derail an opponent's attack or reset aggro.

2. CHARACTER CLASSES & PARTY MEMBERS
1. Eric Huang (The Extortion Economist / Corporate Parasite)
* Role: Logistical Support / Manipulator / Final Boss of the Spotify Arc.
* Passives: WTM Spam (Assesses group vulnerability), Chief of Staff (Controls group logistics).

* Role: High-Motor Operator / DPS / The Engine.

3. Nick Hedgecock (The Strategic Anchor / The Obstructionist)

3. LOCATIONS, DUNGEONS & REALMS
* Rockville (The Hub World): The starting zone. Safe, but boring. Nick F is constantly trying to escape it.
* South Campus Commons Apt 1522 (The Base Camp): The UMD apartment. Features "The Sex Room" (Eric's room), a sink full of rotting dishes, and Zander (an unwanted roommate NPC).
* Shepherd University (The Exile Zone): Jacob's distant domain. Reaching it requires a 1-hour drive. Enemies here include "Athletes" and "The Alt-Girl." Note: Spelling it "Shepard" deals damage to Jacob.
* The Liminal Spaces (Late-Night Dungeons):
    * Dogwood Park: A frequent smoke spot.
    * Maryland Alleys / Robert Frost Sledding Hill: Dungeons that only unlock when it snows.
* The Food Court (Vendors/Taverns):
    * Honeypig: High-tier dining, requires high gold.
    * Hot Lola's: Mid-tier vendor.
    * Cstones / Looneys Pub: Nightlife hubs. "Looney Tuesdays" is a weekly raid event.
* The Impossible Realms (Vaporware Locations): Places the group plans to visit but never reaches due to inertia or flake-outs. Include: New York City, Medellín (Colombia), Montreal, Ottawa, Japan, and the Cabin.

5. KEY QUESTLINES & STORY ARCS (THE GAUNTLET)
Act I: The Night-Time New York Mishap (Operation Inertia)
* Setup: Nick F initiates a drive to NYC at 1:00 AM. Jacob invests $100.
* Conflict: At the Baltimore checkpoint, Nick H initiates "Bedtime Protocol" and refuses to move forward. He offers a counter-bribe of $40 to turn back.
* Climax: Jacob suffers a "Systemic Melt" over his lost $100. Eric snipes them with cold math ("We lost 90 minutes"). The party must navigate the chaos without breaking the social contract.
Act II: The Spotify Family Insurgency
* Setup: Jordan uncovers the mathematical truth: Eric is charging $4.50 for a plan that costs $3.33.
* Conflict: A rebellion begins. Maharko attempts to start a competing labor union/family plan.
* Boss Fight: Eric Huang. He uses Corporate Gaslighting and blames "Joe Biden and Inflation." He deflects using the Apple Music Audit.
* Resolution: Eric wins because he holds the admin passwords.
Act III: The Shepherd Siege & The "Chopped" Off
* Setup: The group drives an hour to visit Jacob at his college. Jacob uses Weaponized Delusion to claim they aren't there ("That's Google Maps").
* Side Quest (The Chopped Off): Jacob meets Makayla and Audrey. The party must rank the girls based on the "Chopped" metric. Jacob constantly fumbles the bag and adopts his "Sub-Zero" persona.
Act IV: The AI Car Cyber-Warfare
* Setup: Eric, Nick F, and Alex wage digital warfare against Maharko.
* Mechanic: Players must use AI image generators to superimpose better cars (C55 AMG, Tesla) over Maharko's Instagram posts to "mog" him.
* Counter-Attack: Maharko retaliates with "The D1 Consumerism Callout" ("holy consumer 💀").
Act V: The Cabin Collapse (Endgame)
* Setup: The planning of the 2026 Cabin Trip.
* Conflict: Nick F creates a Google Doc with 9 options. Chaos ensues over beds, toilets, and random invites (including 15-year-olds and random NPCs).
* The Twist: Nick F abruptly cancels the trip to fly to Spain for his girlfriend, pocketing the refunds. The group must decide whether to go to Japan, Key West, or completely dissolve.
