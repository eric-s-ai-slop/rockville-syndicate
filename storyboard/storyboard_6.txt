DESIGN & STORYBOARD EXTRACTION: PART 7 (THE FINAL SCRAPE)
34. THE EQUIPMENT META: "NICHE FRAGRANCES" & AURA
Armor isn't enough in this game. To succeed in social encounters, players must equip "Fragrances" to manipulate NPC aggro and increase their "Aura" stat.
* The Mechanic: Players can purchase colognes from the "eBay Black Market" or official vendors. Equipping them grants passive buffs but drains massive amounts of gold.
* Item: "Mancera Red Tobacco Intense"
    * Stats: +50 Aura, -100 Gold.
    * Lore: Nick F’s signature scent. He claims it makes him the "best smelling gangster of the whole gc."
* Item: "Last Birthday Cake by Toskovat"
    * Stats: Grants the Gourmand buff (smells like cake).
    * Lore: A highly contested item. Nick F hoards this to "invest in girls."
* The Dollar Store Cologne (Jacob's Debuff):
    * If playing as Jacob, the player can buy an $8 cologne. However, if Nick F or Eric cast Forensic Sniff, they will instantly detect the cheap price tag, inflicting the [Public Shame] debuff on Jacob.
35. AUDIO WARFARE: THE "PHONK" TORTURE SYSTEM
Music is not just background noise; it is a weapon of psychological attrition used exclusively during the Cabin/Road Trip sequences.
* The "Ultraphonk" & "Hyperphonk" Spells:
    * Casters: Eric and Alex
    * Effect: AoE (Area of Effect) psychological damage to the entire party.
    * Condition: The spell Hyperphonk can only be cast after 3:00 AM.
    * Lore/Dialogue: "Y'all already weren't gonna be allowed to sleep. Now NO one is sleeping. ALL 4 days." It completely disables the party's ability to use "Rest" to restore HP.
* The Apple Music Inquisition (Faction Loyalty):
    * Nick F operates as a Paladin for the Apple Music faction. He will relentlessly attempt to convert Spotify users.
    * Dialogue Trap: Nick F will offer the service for only $2. If the player accepts, he attacks with a barrage of buzzwords: "Dolby atmos, lossless audio, enhanced airpods support! It even has a Windows app!" Yielding to him causes Jordan to suffer Playlist Loss anxiety.
36. WARDROBE QUESTS: JESTERMAXXING & "LOONEY TUESDAYS"
Clothing choices directly alter reality and grant access to hidden vendor tiers.
* The "Looney Tuesdays" Cult Protocol:
    * To enter the Cstones/Looneys Pub dungeon on a Tuesday, Nick F demands the player dress "URBAN."
    * If Jacob equips the [Country Formal] set (Denim jacket and boots), he is barred from entry.
    * The "Tipsy Tuesday" Exploit: Nick H gaslights Jacob into believing the dress code requires a bathing suit. If Jacob equips swimwear, his Social Standing drops to zero.
* "Jestermaxxing": A specific stance Jacob can activate by sending awkward mirror selfies to the group. Nick F will respond with "So handsome!" to artificially inflate Jacob's ego before setting him up for a catastrophic fall.
37. FACTIONS & GUILDS: THE "BELI" ELITE & SUBLEASING
The game features esoteric guilds that players must navigate to survive the UMD Basecamp.
* The "Beli" App Syndicate:
    * Eric and Nick H will randomly spam the UI with "Beli" invite links (a restaurant tracking app).
    * Quest: The player must accept the invite within a 24-hour ticking clock. Failing to do so restricts the player from joining "Food Court" raids (e.g., Honeypig, Hot Lola's).
* The Aidan Room Sublease (Negotiation Minigame):
    * Setup: NPC Aidan is leaving, creating a vacant room in Commons 1522.
    * The Haggling: The base rent is $1050. Jacob (The Whale) attempts to lowball at $750, stating, "I'm not living under a communist building. That's how the market works."
    * Resolution: Eric simply steals the room ("I'm migrating to Aidan's room"), bypassing the financial negotiation entirely and leaving Jacob stranded at Shepherd.
38. THE MIDNIGHT GHOST OF 12 WATCHWATER WAY
An ultra-rare, high-danger encounter tied to the Blacklisted NPC, Ben Bersofsky.
* The Location: 12 Watchwater Way (The Pariah Zone).
* The Go-Kart Apparition:
    * Event: At exactly 12:38 AM in the UMD parking lot, the player has a 5% chance to encounter Ben aggressively racing a go-kart in complete silence.
    * Dialogue: If approached, he simply states: "Ben Ber. Racing never stops."
* The "Suspicious Stew" Assimilation: If the player stays too long in Ben's proximity, they ingest the "Stew" and undergo a permanent class-change into the "Alchemist of Suspicious Stew," adopting Ben's erratic, threatening communication style.
39. TICKETMASTER TRENCH WARFARE (THE TAME IMPALA RAID)
A high-stress Quick Time Event (QTE).
* The Event: Tame Impala announces "The Deadbeat Tour" in Baltimore (July 18, 2026).
* The Mechanic: Eric must battle the Ticketmaster UI. The player must mash buttons to bypass the digital queue.
* The Financial Leverage: If Nick F secures the tickets first, he charges the group via Zelle with a mandatory 5% interest fee.
40. THE "ARE YOU 291 LIQUID?" GATEKEEPING CHECK
A recurring dialogue check used to establish dominance.
* The Lore: Before any major trip (Medellín, Montreal, The Cabin), Nick F will halt the party and demand: "Are you 291 liquid?"
* The Response Tree:
    * Eric's Response: Checks his brokerage, realizes he only has $250, and demands someone front him $40.
    * Jacob's Response: Arrogantly claims "I'm 13x that," completely missing the point of the question, which is about willingness to spend, not capacity to spend.
41. THE CHRIS RIVAS "PHANTOM ALIBI" SYSTEM
A deception mechanic exclusively available to Jacob.
* The Skill: When the group chat demands to know Jacob's location, he can cast [Summon Chris Rivas].
* Effect: Jacob claims he is hanging out with Chris Rivas to appear socially active.
* Counter-play: The Syndicate can roll a Forensic Audit. If they realize Chris Rivas is just a guy whose only notable lore is possessing the "6th grade phone number" of a suicidal RM student, Jacob's bluff shatters, and he is exposed as sitting alone at Montgomery Mall.
