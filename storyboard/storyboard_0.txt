7. ADVANCED SYSTEMS & MINIGAMES
A. The Market Exchange (Financial Meta-Game)
Dev Note: Gold shouldn't just sit in an inventory. It rots due to the "Inflation Tax" (-4% purchasing power per in-game year).
The Stock Market (Solvents): Players can buy $PLTR, $AAPL, or short Apple. Nick F constantly pushes "Copper" as real gold. Eric generates passive "Alpha" through the Spotify extortion.
The Crypto Casino (Speculators): Players can buy $SOL or Meme Coins. This is high-risk/high-reward. If Jordan or Maharko are in the party, they act as "liquidity providers" (they always buy high and sell low).
Checking Accounts (Bleeders): Jacob's strategy. He boasts about having a "$3,900 Liquid Bankroll" but refuses to deploy it, taking continuous inflation damage.
B. The "AI Slop" Forge (Crafting System)
Mechanic: Instead of crafting potions, players craft "AI Slop." Eric and Nick F trained custom ChatGPT models of Jacob and Nick H.
Usage: In combat, players can deploy an AI clone of a party member to predict enemy moves or generate a "Truth Nuke" to shatter an opponent's delusion.
The "AI Car" Upgrade: Players can generate fake images of themselves next to C55 AMGs or Teslas to permanently lower an enemy's social standing (Aura).
C. The "Sleep Goblin" & Fatigue Meter
Mechanic: Every character has a hidden "Decay Rate."
Implementation: Nick Hedgecock’s stamina drops precipitously past 10:00 PM. If his fatigue meter fills, he enters "Sleep Goblin" mode. To keep him in the party, players must craft a "Napkin labeled 'For the Sleep Goblin'" containing bribe money.
The "I'm Tired" Bailout: A defensive spell. Can be cast to exit any raid, but leaves the caster vulnerable to hypocrisy damage if they are caught playing GamePigeon Crazy 8s at 2:00 AM immediately after casting it.
8. CRITICAL SIDE QUESTS & RAID EVENTS
Side Quest: The Jungle Gym Gambit (The Three Rules)
Quest Giver: Nick Hedgecock
Objective: Trick Jacob into arriving at a mysterious location (1202 Princeton Pl) without telling him what it is.
Dialogue Puzzle: Nick H issues "The Three Rules" to Jacob:
I sit in my seat.
?
I smoke whatever you give me.
Win Condition: Players must spam the 🐔 (Chicken) emoji to trigger Jacob's FOMO, forcing him to show up to the location blind.
Raid Event: Operation Ding Dong Ditch Ben
Target: 12 Watchwater Way (The Pariah Zone).
Lore: Ben Bersofsky is a blacklisted former party member (due to the "UMBC Pariah Event").
Execution: A stealth raid. The party must pull up to his house at midnight. Maharko must deliver the finishing blow: shouting "WE KNOW WHAT YOU DID" to shatter Ben's final social mask before sprinting back to the getaway car (the Tucson or C55).
Side Quest: The Suspicious Stew Salvage
Quest Giver: Benjamin (Pre-Blacklist) / Maharko
Objective: Ben drops two plastic Tupperwares of a mysterious, glowing "Stew" in a parking lot.
Mechanic: The player must quickly play a minigame to scoop the surviving stew into a new container. Consuming the stew causes the player's personality to permanently assimilate into a Ben/Maharko variant. Dev Note: Treat the stew as a sentient, radioactive item in the inventory.
World Event: The UMD Power Outage (Liminal Spelunking)
Trigger: A random "balloon" hits a power line, shutting off electricity to the entire University of Maryland map.
Event: School is canceled. The map plunges into darkness, becoming a "Liminal Space." The party must navigate the pitch-black campus to hotbox Zander's empty room while avoiding the RA.
9. EXPANDED NPC ROSTER & SUMMONS
1. Audrey (The 10-Year Phantom Objective)
Role: The Unattainable Holy Grail for Jacob.
Lore: She lives in Canada, has a boyfriend, and only responds in two-word sentences. Jacob has mathematically calculated via "Universal Math" that they will marry in 10 years.
Mechanic: If Audrey texts Jacob, the entire party must enter an "Emergency Meeting" to peer-review his text response. Failing the dialogue tree causes Audrey to leave him on read for 3 months.
2. Makayla (The "Chopped" Rebound)
Role: The 17/18-year-old Shepherd University student.
Lore: Jacob attempts to pivot to her, leading to the "Chopped-Off" debate (the party arguing over whether she is ugly).
Outcome: She ultimately rejects Jacob after he sits in her room in silence for two hours while she doom-scrolls on her phone.
3. Caleb Allentuck (The Alt-Infiltrator)
Role: Status: Emerging Threat / Summon.
Ability: "Rave Conditioning." Caleb can be summoned to provide infinite stamina to the party.
Passive: "Brainrot Shield." Emits an aura of Gen Alpha slang (Skibidi, Rizz, Ohio) that deflects all logical attacks from high-IQ enemies like Eric.
Hazard: If Caleb gets too close to Ben Bersofsky, they trigger a "Galaxy Gas Feedback Loop," severely damaging the party's brain cells (permanently lowering BIQ).
4. Zander (The Unwanted Roommate)
Role: Environmental Hazard.
Lore: He is assigned to take over Eric's room in the UMD apartment. The party treats his very existence as an invasion.
Interaction: Players can use the spell Hotbox Zander's Room to assert dominance and claim his territory.

The Setup: Nick Farrar (The Kinetic Warlord) hits peak momentum. He renames the group chat to "DECADES" and demands the entire party deploy to the DECADES nightclub in DC.
The Fracture: Eric Huang (The Apex Predator/Spammer of "WTM") suddenly halts all momentum. He refuses the quest with a lethal Truth Nuke: "I can't. And I'm not going to decades 💀. ts is buns."

The Fallout: The game enters a state of Limbo. The UI glitches. The player is forced to decide the future of the Syndicate:
