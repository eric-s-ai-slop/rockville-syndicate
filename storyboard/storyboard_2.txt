DESIGN & STORYBOARD EXTRACTION: PART 3
11. MOUNTS & THE VEHICULAR TRAVEL SYSTEM
Fast travel isn’t free. The party must maintain their fleet, each with unique stats and drawbacks.
The C55 AMG (Nick F's Mount):
Stats: Extremely high speed, maximum "Aura."
Debuffs: Requires frequent maintenance. Can suffer catastrophic "Frame Damage." Often triggers the "Keys Locked Inside" delay event.
Buff: "Unlimited Range" (If equipped, ignores all logic regarding distance and gas money).
The Hyundai Tucson / "Mommy Mobile" (Nick H's Mount):
Stats: Reliable, holds 5 party members.
Debuff: Requires "Mommy's" permission to deploy. Reduces party's overall street cred.
The GLI / Jetta (Jordan's Mount):
Stats: The backup reserve vehicle. Rarely deployed unless absolutely necessary.
The Radar Detector (Vehicle Accessory):
Mechanic: While driving to Ocean City or Liminal Spaces, the UI flashes "KA BAND." A mini-game triggers where the driver must reduce speed before Eric shouts "COP CHEVY TAHOE BLACK." Failing results in a massive gold penalty (speeding ticket).
12. THE FOG OF WAR: "FIND MY" SURVEILLANCE
Information is power. The game features a live-tracking mechanic representing the "Find My" app.
Location Sharing: Characters must opt-in to share locations. Eric operates under permanent stealth ("UR NEVER GETTING MY LOCATION").
The "EZ" Hack: Maharko (The Tracker) possesses a bypass skill. If a character hides their location, Maharko can roll a cyber-warfare check to drop a screenshot of their exact coordinates into the chat, inflicting the Exposed status effect.
The "I'm Turning Around" Bluff: Jacob frequently claims he is turning his car around to go home. The party can check "Find My" to call his bluff, dealing critical psychological damage.
13. BASE CAMP MANAGEMENT: COMMONS APT 1522
When not in the Rockville Hub, the party rests at the UMD Apartment. Base management is required to maintain party morale.
The Dishwasher Raid:
Event: A persistent environmental hazard. Dirty plates and forks accumulate in the sink.
Conflict: Eric triggers an "Emergency Meeting" demanding users rinse solids off before loading. Nick H refuses ("I'm not having 25 different forks in the sink").
Consequence: If the sink reaches maximum capacity, Eric gains the Enraged debuff and refuses to participate in "WTM" events.
Random Campus Disasters:
The Steam Outage: UMD loses heat. The apartment drops to "a calm 63 degrees," forcing players to equip winter gear indoors.
The Business School Fire: A random overworld event that cancels classes, temporarily boosting Nick F's happiness stats.
14. THE GRAND CABIN BETRAYAL ARC (MAJOR QUESTLINE)
This is the longest, most complex questline in the game, spanning months of in-game time. It ultimately ends in betrayal.
Phase 1: The Google Doc Inquisition: Nick F drops a 9-option list of Airbnbs. The party must vote. Players must filter out insane suggestions (like the "Toilet Cabin" with 1 bathroom for 15 people).
Phase 2: The Roster Bloat: Nick F attempts to invite random NPCs (Josh, Rocco, Anthony, the 15-year-old H2O girls, and the UMBC victims). The player must use persuasion to pare the list down to the core 8 members.
Phase 3: The Extortion: Nick F demands precisely $307.44125 from each member via Zelle or Robinhood Crypto transfers. If players refuse, they are threatened with a 2% interest penalty.
Phase 4: The Betrayal (Agent Buybacks): After collecting the gold, Nick F abruptly cancels the Cabin Trip. He uses the party's pooled funds to buy a flight to Spain ("International Business") to visit his girlfriend Emily.
The Aftermath: The party enters a state of mourning. They cope by playing Ultraphonk and Hyperphonk music at 3:00 AM, and consider booking revenge trips to Japan or Key West.
15. JACOB’S SURVIVAL ARCS (CHARACTER QUESTS)
Quest: The Medical Near-Miss (The Rejuvenation)
Event: Jacob suddenly loses 20 lbs and drops to 1 HP. He is hospitalized (Day 4.5) and diagnosed with untreated diabetes.
The Party's Response: Instead of sympathy, the party jokes about him missing McDonald's. Eric suggests finding the mythical "Frederick Rejuvenation Syrup" to heal him.
Reward: Surviving this grants Jacob the "Lebby Redemption Arc" buff, increasing his confidence.
The "Rizzology" Skill Tree & The Chopped Off
Mechanic: Jacob must navigate complex dialogue trees with female NPCs (Makayla, Audrey, and a random alt-girl on Hinge).
The "Touch Barrier" Check: Jacob asks the party what constitutes breaking the touch barrier. Eric responds: "It's head."
The Alignment Choice: The party asks Jacob if he is a "Lover boy" or a "Play boy." Jacob selects "Lover boy," but his actions lean toward "Jestermaxxing."
The 12-Year-Old Catfish Hazard: Jacob accidentally talks to a 12-year-old on Discord. He must hit the Block button before Nick H reports him to the authorities or adds him to the "Tea App."
The $1,500 Bet (Legendary Contract)
Jacob locks in a legally binding, 10-year contract. If he marries Audrey within a decade, he wins. If he fails, he owes the party $1,500. This operates as a persistent ticking clock in the background of the entire game.
16. THE INVENTORY: APPS & DIGITAL ARTIFACTS
The players use specific apps to trigger events and manipulate the world.
Spotify Admin Privileges: Eric holds the master key. He can threaten to migrate the party to Apple Music. Nick F aggressively shills Apple Music ("Dolby atmos, lossless audio!").
Letterboxd / Beli Invites: Nick H spams the chat with app referral codes. Accepting them grants him VIP status, ignoring them causes his "Resentment" meter to fill.
The Unsent Project Archive: An investigation mini-game. Jacob finds an anonymous love letter from July 5th addressed to "Lebby" and convinces himself it's from Audrey. Nick F uses Photoshop to prove it's fake.
Robinhood Links: Eric spams referral links to get free stock. The party debates buying $DJT or $PLTR, while Jordan recklessly drops his car loan into $SOL right before a 40% market crash.
17. FINAL COMBAT & STATUS EFFECTS
Status: "Jestermaxxing" – A state where a character acts like a clown to impress women. Reduces respect from the Rockville Core but slightly increases NPC engagement.
Status: "Gooning" – A debuff applied when a character stays home alone watching Netflix or playing Fortnite instead of attending a WTM.
Status: "Sub-Zero" – Jacob's ultimate defensive stance. Activated when rejected. He becomes emotionally numb, turning his text bubbles cold and distant ("Ice Cold. Sub zero moment").
The "Block" Ultimate: A move used constantly by Jacob and Eric. It temporarily removes a player from the party UI. It is easily countered by the "Add Back" passive skill.
