THE SUDS & SOLES POOL PARTY: THE TRUE STORY
The Build-Up and Logistics
On Saturday, June 13, 2026, Nick F decided to throw a pool party. He wanted as many people to come as possible, but realizing he didn't want to handle the heavy lifting of gathering the crowd, he offloaded the logistics to Nick H and Eric. Eric, who had been spending the afternoon messing around with Gemini Pro 3.1 AI agents, took charge of organization but actively decided to withhold his "motion." He had the network to bring people, but he chose not to bring anyone himself.
Instead, it was Nick’s girlfriend, Anastasia, who pushed through the gatekeeping. She took the initiative and invited her friend Sophia, bringing the female headcount to four.
Meanwhile, Nick F was stressing in the group chat. The pool was sitting at a remarkably warm 87 degrees, and the supply list was severely lacking. He was rapid-firing questions in the chat asking if anyone had chips, Red Bulls, ice, or hotdogs. Eric stepped up to buy 20 lbs of ice to cool the water down, and Nick F managed to secure the hotdogs (the "Franks").
The Suds & Soles Blockade
While everyone else was getting ready, Jacob was complaining in the chat. The annual Rockville "Suds & Soles" 5K marathon was running directly past his house, completely blocking his street with police and thousands of runners. He insisted he couldn't leave, telling the group he was just eating dinner with his grandma instead.
Around 8:12 PM, Jacob was on the verge of bailing entirely to stay home and watch basketball, partly because he was annoyed about Nick F's potential "bathroom rules." But then, the dynamic shifted. Jacob asked the chat who was there.
Nick H replied: "The boys and girls."
Jacob immediately zeroed in: "Girls? Are they single?"
Eric confirmed that they were. Suddenly, Jacob's attitude changed, the streets miraculously opened up, and he announced he was on his way.
The Arrival & The Disappointment
The core crew was already at the house having a great time. Sam Ferretti was also there, but he wasn't feeling well, so he took a step back and quietly sat out on the sidelines to spectate. Eric's 20 lbs of ice had successfully cooled the main pool, and Nick F was grilling up the Franks, which ended up saving everyone from starving.
Around 9:00 PM, Jacob finally pulled up. Before the party, Nick F had given Jacob one very specific job: bring four Peach Red Bulls. Jacob arrived completely empty-handed on that front. He didn't bring the Red Bulls, but he did bring his own personal supply of beer for himself. Adding to the confusion, he didn't even bring swim trunks.
Rather than blowing up at Jacob for ignoring the one thing he was asked to do, Nick F just took it on the chin. He didn't start a fight; he just absorbed the silent disappointment and let it go to keep the party moving.
The Hot Tub and The 10% Scale
Later in the evening, the group migrated to the hot tub, where they had set up a projector. For the first part of the night, they watched the Knicks game from the jacuzzi, and the vibes were great. But once the game ended, the entertainment switched to the infamous gay hockey romance, Heated Rivalry.
This is when Jacob became the loudest person at the party. Sitting in the hot tub as the show played on the projector, he started giving a constant, highly un-politically correct commentary. Out of nowhere, he began publicly calculating his own sexuality on a sliding mathematical scale. He announced to the party that he was starting at "1%," but as the show went on, he claimed he was currently at "4% gay right there." However, he made sure to assure everyone that his absolute, mathematical upper bound could never exceed 10%. Sam Ferretti, still not feeling well, just watched this entire unhinged rant unfold from the patio.
The "Urban" Jump
After hours of hanging around in his street clothes and sitting in the hot tub, Jacob finally decided he wanted to jump into the main pool. But since he didn't bring a swimsuit, he just jumped into the water wearing his normal shorts.
Strangely, he also refused to take off his hat, keeping his cap on while fully submerged in the water because he thought it made him look "urban." He did this fully expecting it to get a reaction, or at least some attention, from Anastasia and Sophia.
It backfired completely. The girls simply did not acknowledge him. They didn't laugh, they didn't make a comment—they just completely ignored the fact that he was treading water in soaking wet street clothes and a baseball cap.

He also bet 3 dollars on the knicks winning when they were down 10 points, and when the Knicks came back and won, he was like "I TOLD YOU GUYS" and he said how he was a knicks fan now and that he would go to new york.
The Aftermath
Despite Jacob's missing Red Bulls, the 5K marathon delays, and the un-PC commentary, the night was a massive success. The Franks and the ice hit perfectly, the hot tub projector setup worked, and the next morning at 1:40 AM, Eric dropped the final verdict in the group chat: "that party was so tuff."
