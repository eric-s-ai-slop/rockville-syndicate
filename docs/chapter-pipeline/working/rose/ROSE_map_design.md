## PLAIN ENGLISH PROPOSAL

This is a three-scene chapter. The story physically moves from the party interior → the lawn outside → the car interior. Each location is a distinct dramatic beat and can't share a map with the others.

### Scene 0 — The Party (920×660, apartment theme)

A Florida house party interior. Living room on the left (couch, coffee table, plant, cabinet), kitchen on the right (counter, fridge, sink). A rug in the center floor ties the two spaces together. A TV mounted on the back wall. The player enters from the bottom-center.

**Actor placement logic:**
- Maharko is by the couch — he's already spotted Rose and is moving toward her.
- Jordan is right behind Maharko (with him the whole time, per the brief).
- Rose is on the couch — the first target.
- Alex is in the center of the room, between the living room and kitchen. He's the one who'll deliver the age info. Placing him in the center makes him the social hinge of the scene.
- Nick F is near the player spawn, just arrived at the party.
- Benji is in the kitchen area — he brought the girls, he's mingling.
- Rose's sister is also in the kitchen, near the fridge, already drinking ("getting lit").
- Three background partygoers scattered for atmosphere.

No boss arena in this scene. It's the setup — beats 1 and 2 (arrival, ramp).

### Scene 1 — The Lawn (920×660, florida theme)

End of night. Outside the house. The house wall is at the top (background). A grass lawn fills the space. A walkway cuts across the lower portion. A tree on the left for framing.

**Actor placement logic:**
- Rose is on the ground in the center — she's collapsed, "super wasted."
- Maharko is next to her, slightly above — pulling her up.
- Nick F is standing a few steps back and to the right — phone up, taking the photo. This is the lawn photo being born.
- Jordan is beside Maharko, slightly behind — present, watching, saying nothing.
- Alex is nearby to the right — also watching, having already backed down.

Rose's sister is NOT in this scene — she's still inside, "getting lit," unaware.

No boss arena here either. This is beat 3 — the lawn photo, the chapter's turn.

### Scene 2 — The Car (920×660, highway_night theme)

The closed system. The car interior fills the map. Windshield strip at the top (dark, implied movement). Two front seats (Nick F driving, Jordan passenger). A solid divider between front and back seats blocks the player from walking forward — the player is trapped in the back. Two back seats (Maharko left, Rose incapacitated next to him, Alex right). Locked car doors on the left and right walls (solid — you can't open them). Rear window strip at the bottom.

**Actor placement logic:**
- Nick F in the driver's seat (front left). The archivist is behind the wheel.
- Jordan in the front passenger seat (front right). The silent auditor faces forward, away from the action. The two observers are in the front, physically separated from the back seat drama — thematically perfect.
- Maharko in the back left seat.
- Rose next to Maharko, incapacitated. She's placed between Maharko and the center of the back seat.
- Alex in the back right seat. He's the one who'll say stop. He's as far from Maharko as the back seat allows.

The player spawns in the back seat open floor, between Alex and center — close enough to the action to be implicated, far enough to have chosen to be there.
