## PASS 1 — CHRONOLOGY

**Pre-history: the vacancy (canon tie: chapter3b / ben_umbc brief)**
- The group used to make fun of Ben "the same way" — night hangouts as punching down.
- After the Ben/UMBC incident, the group distanced from Ben. Result (Eric's words): they
  "didn't have a punching bag anymore." That vacancy is WHY they wanted Jacob.
- TIMING RESOLVED: they stopped talking to Ben ~a year before the Jacob event (~2023);
  the formal exile landed around the same period as summer 2024 but definitely BEFORE
  the McDonald's night. ⇒ The vacancy was freshly sealed when Jacob walked in. The slow
  fade preceded the verdict; Jacob arrives just after the verdict.

**Summer 2024 — the McDonald's night**
- Rising-sophomore college summer, right before Maharko left for Florida.
- Nick F, Nick H, Maharko: bored, hanging out at night, probably smoking. The three were
  already inside; **Jacob and Chris walked in** (~2–3am).
- Status geometry (Eric's read, Pass 2 material): the Nicks considered Maharko "cool"
  and were trying to impress him. Maharko's presence made them punch down HARDER — the
  cruelty was an **audition performed upward**. Two ladders in one room: Nicks climbing
  toward Maharko by stepping on Jacob; Jacob (later) climbing by stepping on Chris.
- No verbatim dialogue survives; what's canonical is the TONE — condescension, acting
  better than him.
- Intended as one-time. ⚠️ Eric thinks there was probably ONE more pre-calls encounter
  with Jacob — pin down or cut.

**The telling (a few weeks later) — a scene in its own right**
- The Nicks told Eric as a trophy/funny offhand story. The core joke as told: "why is
  Jacob at McDonald's at 2am, so weird, what a loser" — **while they had also been at
  McDonald's at 2am.** The joke indicts the tellers; the only difference between loser
  and legend in this world is who controls the narrative. (Scene-defining irony — the
  prose draft should let the player notice this before anyone on screen does.)

**A few weeks later — Eric hears about it**
- Told by the two Nicks. They "talked down on them and called them losers."
- Eric's stated motive for intervening: **his own entertainment** — "I thought that I
  could milk a lot more content out of Jacob than just letting them meet him once."
  (Pass 5 note: interrogate this framing — content-farming as the admin's origin story.)

**The double-call era (summer 2024 → ~Feb 2025)**
- MECHANIC RESOLVED: real tool = **Prank Hotline** (⚠️ keep anonymous in-game —
  fictionalize the name). Takes two phone numbers, rings both, and bridges them so each
  side sees an incoming call it didn't place. Neither party dialed; both received.
  ⭐ CRITICAL SUB-MECHANIC (the voicemail trap): if one side doesn't pick up, the other is
  connected to that person's voicemail. Since each believed the OTHER had called them,
  reaching voicemail read as the other person deliberately dodging / running a bit. The
  tool's dead-air failure state was reinterpreted as intent. (See `02_primary_sources.md`
  Oct 10 — the group praised Jacob for "creative" behavior that was literally just his
  voicemail playing to a line he never dialed.)
- First call was NOT in the dorm — it was during the **summer**, right after Eric heard
  the story. (Correction to earlier note.) Eric was not present for outcomes; Nick F
  would text the GC about each call afterward. → The GROUP CHAT is a recurring stage:
  Eric's window into a prank he runs blind.
- Frequency: ~once a week on average across the era; bursty (some weeks many, some none).
- Failure mode that became the best material: Jacob often **didn't pick up**, so Nick F's
  line just sat silent. The group read the silence as Jacob "calling for attention then
  saying nothing." The silence INCRIMINATED Jacob rather than exposing the trick.
- The "who called who" argument DID happen — repeatedly. Jacob tried to prove he'd also
  gotten an incoming call. The group's low opinion of him meant they disregarded his
  proof every time. **The trick was protected by contempt, not by cleverness.** (This is
  a thesis-grade fact — the prank only worked because they'd already decided Jacob was
  beneath belief.)
- Jacob's side: still unknown to Eric; probably the mirror belief. "Someone is messing
  with us" was the true explanation and the one nobody would entertain.

**First real hangout**
- Same/similar shape to the McDonald's night: invited Jacob out at night, gave him a
  joint. Dogwood Park baseball lookout building → drive to McDonald's.
- Register of these hangouts: still punching down — Jacob as Ben's replacement.
- Eric knew Jacob from high school / childhood, but the **first face-to-face of this era**
  was this first post-calls hangout at Dogwood. So: Eric spent the entire double-call era
  operating on someone he'd known for years, remotely, without seeing him. (Strong scene:
  Eric and Jacob's first in-person meeting of the era, Eric knowing exactly what he'd
  been doing, Jacob knowing nothing.)
- ⚠️ Maharko in Florida by now? Was he part of the Jacob hangouts at all, or only the
  original McDonald's night before leaving?

**The fade of Chris Rivas**
- Chris doesn't show up after the McDonald's night; group never interacted with him
  again. Jacob mentions him only in passing — claims he doesn't hang out with him
  anymore. Eric suspects Jacob says this to sound cooler (Jacob thought Chris was the
  bigger loser) and that Jacob still hung out with him some.
- Thematic note: Jacob punching down at Chris to climb — the ladder recurses one rung

**The vacancy, refined**
- The group wasn't consciously "searching" for a Ben replacement — that's Eric's read,

- CORRECTION (2026-07-06): Nick H did NOT actually become a punching bag — he got ribbed,

**End of the origin era**
- No clean end. The bit never fully stopped — Jacob is still a punching bag, just one with
