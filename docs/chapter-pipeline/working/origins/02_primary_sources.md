## THE FOUNDING-ERA GOLD (verbatim)

**Oct 6 2024 — the trick working, seen from inside (electric vehicle squad):**
> Nick F: "Bro Ben just called me"
> Eric: "why"  "💀"
> Nick F: "Apperantly Jacob is spamming him and he thinks were causing it"
> Nick F: "Bro Jacob is a LOSER"

Note the blast radius: the double-calls reached Ben (the *previous* punching bag), who
called Nick F to complain that Jacob was spamming him and that he thought the group was
behind it. Ben was RIGHT — Eric was behind it — and even the correct accusation gets
buried under "Jacob is a LOSER." Eric plays dumb ("why 💀") to the group while running
the machine. Peak dramatic-irony scene, and it's real. CONFIRMED: Eric knew in the moment
his calls had reached Ben (he was reading the same thread) — so the dumb-play is a
conscious cover, not hindsight. He watched the wrong man take the fall in real time and
typed "💀".

**Oct 10 2024 — the phantom cleverness (electric vehicle squad):**
> Nick F: "He just called me and had a voice script to pretend i went to his voicemail"
> Nick H: "at least he was creative"
> Nick H: "but how do you not have anything better to do"

⭐ CORRECTED MECHANIC (Eric, 2026-07-06): Jacob did NOTHING here. He simply didn't pick
up. Prank Hotline bridges the two numbers; when Jacob didn't answer, Nick F was connected
to **Jacob's actual voicemail** — but because Nick believed *Jacob* had called *him*, he
read the voicemail as a deliberate "script" Jacob rigged to fake-out that Nick had gone
to voicemail. The group then credited Jacob for creativity he never had. **The group
invented Jacob's ingenuity out of a technical artifact.** Even his one moment of respect
in the whole era was a phantom — praise for a thing that didn't happen, aimed at a person
who wasn't there. This is the single most Ferrante fact in the archive and a perfect
playable-irony beat: the player (as Eric) knows the voicemail is just an empty line;
everyone on screen builds a personality for Jacob out of dead air.

**Feb 4 2025 — Eric performing the McDonald's-loser bit himself (electric vehicle squad):**

>   SEE WALK TO THE FIVE GUYS"
> Eric: "WHO TREKS TO MCDONALDS AT 3 AM"

"The Big L" = the group's nickname for Jacob (the Loser). Confirms the McDonald's-at-3am
mockery ran as a live bit into Feb 2025 — the same month the calls wound down. Eric is
not just the secret operator; he's an on-the-record participant in the cruelty. (Pass 5:
this complicates the "I did it for content, not malice" framing — receipts show him
running the bit for the group, warmly.)

**Nov 19 2024 — the "Jacob fiction" (DM with Nick F):**
Eric generated an AI "fiction about a person named jacob lebby" and workshopped it in the
DM. ⚠️ SENSITIVE: it's a cruel transition-fiction naming Jacob. This is real and shows
the register of the era's humor, but it is exactly the material the chapter must decide

**Aug 13 2025 — "Jacob 2.0" GPT (DM with Nick F):**
The group later built a chatbot clone of Jacob ("You will be a chatbot clone of this
person named Jacob Lebby… indistinguishable as talking to him in person"). Shows the bit

mockery had fully fused.
