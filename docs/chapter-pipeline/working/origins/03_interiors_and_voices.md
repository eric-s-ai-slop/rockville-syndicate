## ERIC — before "The Admin"

### A) Interior

**Wanted:** Entertainment, on the record and in his own words — "I thought that I could
milk a lot more content out of Jacob than just letting them meet him once." Not status,
not the group's gratitude (nobody knew), not even the outcome. He wanted a *show*. He'd
known Prank Hotline for years before he had a use for it (bible: "the tool predates the
motive... a man holding a hammer who finally saw a nail") — so what he wanted, precisely,
was a worthy application for a capability he'd been privately carrying. The Maria Brooke
pattern, recursed: build a fiction, watch a real person live inside it.

**Afraid of:** The record shows no stated fear, which is itself the point — this is Eric
before Eric has anything to lose. ⚠️ Inferred candidate fears, in descending confidence:
(1) that the content dries up — the operational fear of a showrunner; (2) exposure — his
machine was one glance at a call log away from collapse at the in-person argument, and
the bible is explicit that "the trick was protected by contempt, not by cleverness,"
i.e. by a shield Eric didn't build and couldn't control; (3) ⚠️ least grounded but most
Pass-5-relevant: that it wasn't funny — that he was alone in a room causing calls he
couldn't hear, and the only laughter was secondhand.

**Did NOT know (that others knew, or that no one knew):** Jacob's entire side — bible:
"Jacob's side: still unknown to Eric." He ran an eight-month operation on a person he'd
known since childhood without once hearing a call or seeing his face until Dogwood. All
his feedback was Nick F's GC dispatches. He is the best-informed person in the story and
he is operating *blind*, on hearsay about his own prank. He also did not know — could
not have — that the machine would produce a friendship, a group chat, a capital city
("Sub Zero Squad"), and eventually this game.

**Who he was before the finished character:** Present-day Eric needs the admin title
because "without the admin role, he's just a guy" (DEEPEN). 2024 Eric IS just a guy —
and the archive shows him enjoying it. His masterpiece move on Oct 6 is typing "why" and
"💀" while watching Ben correctly accuse the group of the thing Eric was doing *at that
moment* (source: "CONFIRMED: Eric knew in the moment... the dumb-play is a conscious
cover"). No institution, no ledger, no cover story of service. The admin persona is what
he built LATER — plausibly (⚠️ Pass 5 territory) to retroactively legitimize what this
era taught him: that authoring people's reality feels like mattering, and that he liked
it. The era's Eric has the power without the paperwork. The paperwork comes after, so he
never has to say what the power was for.

receipts, and got nothing for any of it. The causal arc is right there and it's the
cleanest character spine in the chapter: **the Lurker is what you build after an era of
learning that being right, offered upward from the bottom, is worth nothing.** He now
waits, edits, and speaks only when his sentence cannot be brushed aside — because he
once said "no, I will show you the call logs" to a room that had already decided, and
the room laughed. His present-day superpower is the exact behavior his era-self was
punished out of. ⚠️ This causal reading is inference (strong, but Eric should bless it —
it will quietly organize every Jacob line in the chapter).
