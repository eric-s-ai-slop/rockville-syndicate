# The Cursed Cabin Trip of 2025

Every summer, the group took a cabin trip. It was supposed to be a simple tradition: rent a house somewhere remote, buy an irresponsible amount of groceries, and spend several days doing absolutely nothing productive.

For the 2025 trip, however, they decided to make the journey more ambitious. Before retreating into the mountains near Shenandoah, they would spend a few days in Ocean City, Maryland.

The group consisted of Eric, Nick H, Nick F, Jordan, Leo, Alex, Benji, and Maharko. Most of them were turning twenty-one soon, so they decided this would be their final visit to H2O—the under-twenty-one nightclub that had once seemed like the center of Ocean City nightlife.

They arrived expecting one final legendary night.

Instead, H2O was terrible.

Maybe the club had always been terrible and they had simply been younger. Maybe they had finally aged out of enjoying a dark room filled with teenagers, sticky floors, and music loud enough to cause permanent hearing damage. Whatever the reason, the illusion was gone. They stood there realizing, almost simultaneously, that they were now too old for H2O.

Fortunately, they had fake IDs.

The actual bars were much better. The group drank, wandered around Ocean City, and enjoyed what felt like their first taste of real nightlife. Most of them had a good time.

Maharko, however, got no play.

As the nights went on, he became increasingly down bad.

One evening, Maharko and Benji were smoking cigarettes on the balcony when they noticed a group of girls staying one floor above them. The two began talking to them from below, and eventually the groups met up.

Among the girls was one who was extremely drunk, visibly sick, and—to use the group’s preferred terminology—completely chopped. Standing directly beside her was another girl who was noticeably less chopped.

Maharko considered his options.

For reasons nobody could understand, he chose the sick one.

He made out with her.

The decision would have consequences that extended far beyond Ocean City.

By the time the group left, they had learned two important lessons. First, they had officially become too old for H2O. Second, Maharko had apparently contracted some unidentified disease from the balcony girl.

They packed their belongings and began driving toward the cabin, unaware that Ocean City had merely been the opening act.

## The Hospital Detour

The group split between two cars.

Eric drove Alex, Jordan, and Maharko. Nick H drove Leo, Benji, and Nick F.

Eric’s car arrived first.

The cabin looked fine from the outside. It was isolated, surrounded by mountains and trees, with a wooden deck overlooking the forest. At first glance, it seemed like a peaceful place to spend the next several days.

Then they went inside.

The first discovery was that the entire cabin had only one bathroom.

Eight people.

One bathroom.

The second discovery was that there were only two actual bedrooms. Eric and Alex immediately claimed one. Maharko and Jordan claimed the other. This left Nick H, Nick F, Leo, and Benji to arrange themselves around the living room on couches, mattresses, and whatever other surfaces could support a human body.

Eric, Alex, Jordan, and Maharko unpacked, explored the cabin, and waited for the second car to arrive.

An hour passed.

Then another.

Eventually, they checked everyone’s locations.

Nick H’s car was at a hospital.

When they called to ask what had happened, they learned that Leo had developed kidney stones during the drive. Nick H had been forced to stop at an urgent care facility somewhere in the middle of rural Maryland or West Virginia.

The second half of the group would not reach the cabin until around nine or ten that night.

That left Eric’s car responsible for buying all the groceries.

They drove into town, filled their carts, returned to the cabin, and waited. At around ten, Nick H’s car finally pulled into the driveway. Leo had survived the kidney stone incident, but everyone was exhausted from the drive, the hospital, and the general collapse of the day’s schedule.

Nobody had the energy to do anything.

They went to sleep.

The trip had barely begun.

## The Water Situation

The next morning, someone attempted to use the bathroom.

There was no running water.

The cabin had one bathroom for eight people, and that bathroom was now completely unusable.

The sinks did not work. The shower did not work. The toilet could not flush. Anyone who needed to urinate was forced to walk outside and find a suitable tree.

The group contacted the property owner and eventually got the water running again, but only temporarily. For the rest of the trip, the cabin’s water supply would function according to its own mysterious schedule. Sometimes it worked. Sometimes it did not. On an average day, the cabin had running water for perhaps half of the available hours.

Even when the water returned, all eight people still had to share the same bathroom.

Meanwhile, Maharko’s condition was getting worse.

The illness he had acquired from the girl in Ocean City had fully activated. He spent most of the day lying in bed, coughing, sweating, and slowly transforming the bedroom he shared with Jordan into a quarantine zone.

Because Maharko was normally the group’s chef, his illness also created a food crisis. Without him, the group’s culinary abilities collapsed almost immediately. Their meals became variations of grilled burgers, simple snacks, and whatever else could be prepared without expertise.

At one point, Maharko sent Eric, Nick F, and Jordan into town to buy him cold medicine. While they searched the aisles for something powerful enough to revive him, Maharko remained in bed, spreading his Ocean City disease throughout the cabin.

Outside, another problem was developing.

The summer of 2025 was one of the first years when spotted lanternflies seemed to appear everywhere. The cabin’s deck was covered in them. They jumped across the floorboards, landed on people, and made sitting outside deeply unpleasant.

The cabin now had:

* One bathroom for eight people.
* Water that disappeared for half the day.
* A chef confined to bed with an unknown illness.
* A recent kidney stone patient.
* A deck covered in invasive insects.
* Four people sleeping in the living room.

The group persevered.

Mostly because they had already paid for the cabin.

## The First Ultraphunk Attack

Despite the cabin’s many problems, Eric and Alex had secured the best position.

They had their own bedroom.

More importantly, their bedroom had its own air-conditioning unit and a door that could be barricaded.

The people in the living room had none of these advantages.

Eric and Alex decided to exploit the class divide.

They hid a Bluetooth speaker somewhere in the living room and connected to it through an iPad. They waited until everyone outside had settled into their couches and mattresses. The cabin became silent. The lights went out.

Then Ultraphunk began blasting at full volume.

The people in the living room woke up confused and disoriented. Because the cabin was completely dark and the speaker had been hidden, nobody could determine where the music was coming from.

They searched under furniture. They moved blankets. They checked corners. All the while, aggressive phonk music continued shaking the room.

Eventually, they found the speaker.

By then, Eric and Alex had barricaded their bedroom door.

The victims tried to get inside, but the door would not move. Eric and Alex remained safely behind the barricade while the people in the living room shouted threats from the other side.

The first attack was a success.

It would not be the last.

## The Ninety-Degree Living Room

The next morning, Eric opened his bedroom door and walked into the living room.

It was approximately ninety degrees.

Each section of the cabin had its own air-conditioning unit. Eric and Alex had one in their bedroom. Maharko and Jordan had one in the sick room. The living room had another for everyone sleeping outside.

Sometime during the night, the living-room unit had broken.

The four people sleeping there had spent the night trapped in a sweltering room while Eric and Alex enjoyed private air conditioning behind their barricaded door.

The water was also out again.

At this point, Jordan had started becoming sick. This was unsurprising, considering he had been sharing a bedroom—and possibly a bed—with Maharko, the original carrier of the Ocean City plague.

Maharko remained sick. Jordan was becoming sick. Leo had recently suffered kidney stones. The living room was ninety degrees. The bathroom had no water.

The logical response would have been to rest.

Instead, several members of the group decided to do mushrooms.

Jordan, Maharko, Nick F, and Leo all participated. Maharko took cold medicine and mushrooms during the same period, despite already being sick enough to remain in bed for most of the trip.

Eric, Alex, and Benji decided they wanted no involvement in managing four people on mushrooms inside a broken cabin. They left to go hiking.

Nick H stayed behind as the trip sitter.

Unfortunately for Nick F, Nick H did not interpret “trip sitter” to mean “calm and responsible guardian.” He interpreted it to mean “person with unlimited opportunities to troll someone who cannot distinguish reality from imagination.”

Nick F began freaking out.

Nick H made the situation worse on purpose.

At one point, he convinced Nick F that he had peed himself. Nick F panicked and repeatedly checked himself, while Nick H continued reinforcing the lie. Every time Nick F began to calm down, Nick H found another way to confuse him.

This continued for hours.

While the mushroom crisis unfolded, the rest of the cabin’s problems remained active. Benji continued smoking cigarettes in the living room. The water continued disappearing. Lanternflies continued jumping across the deck. Maharko continued coughing and spreading his illness.

The cabin was no longer a vacation property.

It was an uncontrolled medical and psychological experiment.

## The Mattress Attack

That night, Eric and Alex launched their second Ultraphunk operation.

This time, simply hiding the speaker somewhere in the living room was not enough. Their victims had learned from the first attack and would search the obvious locations.

Eric and Alex needed to escalate.

They hid one speaker underneath the pullout mattress.

This meant that the two people sleeping on the mattress would have to wake up, realize that music was coming from beneath them, get out of bed, lift the mattress, and retrieve the speaker.

A second speaker was hidden in a separate corner of the room to create additional confusion.

Once again, Eric and Alex waited until everyone had fallen asleep.

Then both speakers activated.

Ultraphunk blasted from underneath the mattress and from somewhere across the room. The people in the living room woke up in complete confusion. One speaker sounded like it was coming from inside the bed. The other made it difficult to determine the direction of the first.

Blankets were thrown aside. People stood up. The mattress was pulled apart. The room erupted into shouting.

By the time the speakers were located, Eric and Alex were once again sealed behind their barricaded bedroom door.

The following morning, the disease count increased.

Nick F began showing symptoms. Jordan was approaching bed-bound status. Maharko remained almost permanently confined to the sick room.

The trip’s energy collapsed.

Half the group was now ill or recovering from some other medical event. Nobody wanted to cook. Nobody wanted to hike. Nobody wanted to sit outside with the lanternflies. The living room remained uncomfortable, and the water continued disappearing for most of the day.

The group spent another day trapped inside the cursed cabin.

Eric and Alex began planning their final attack.

## The Bathroom Operation

That evening, Eric made an important observation.

The bathroom door could be locked from the inside but unlocked from the outside using a fork.

It was difficult, but possible.

This gave him an idea.

The bathroom was the perfect location for the next speaker. Everyone in the living room could eventually find a speaker hidden under a couch or mattress. But if the speaker were placed inside the bathroom and the bathroom door were locked, they would first have to identify the location of the music and then learn how to defeat the lock.

Eric and Alex placed the speaker inside the bathroom.

They locked the door.

Then they returned to their bedroom and rebuilt the barricade.

The cabin went quiet. Everyone settled into bed.

Ultraphunk began.

The people in the living room woke up for the third consecutive night to the sound of music blasting at full volume.

At first, they had no idea where it was coming from. They searched the room, expecting another speaker under a mattress or behind a couch.

Then someone realized the music was coming from the bathroom.

They tried the handle.

Locked.

This created several stages of confusion.

First, everyone had to wake up fully.

Then they had to understand that the music was real.

Then they had to locate the speaker.

Then they had to realize that the bathroom door was locked.

Finally, they had to determine how to unlock a door that none of them had previously known could be opened with a fork.

The entire process took approximately fifteen minutes.

For those fifteen minutes, Ultraphunk blasted continuously through the cabin.

Eventually, it became an all-hands-on-deck engineering effort. People searched for tools, examined the lock, shouted instructions, and attempted to force the mechanism open.

Behind their barricaded door, Eric and Alex listened to the chaos.

At last, the bathroom was breached and the speaker was silenced.

But the damage had been done.

## Retreat

By the following day, Alex and Leo had started getting sick too.

The Ocean City illness had now spread through most of the cabin. Maharko remained the original carrier and was still largely bed-bound. Jordan had been infected through close-range exposure in the sick room. Nick F had started developing symptoms. Alex and Leo were now joining them.

The cabin still had unreliable water.

The single bathroom still had to be shared by eight people whenever it worked.

The air conditioning in the living room was still broken.

The deck was still occupied by lanternflies.

Nobody was cooking proper meals.

Everyone sleeping in the living room had endured three consecutive nights of Ultraphunk attacks.

The group had finally had enough.

They packed their belongings and left the cabin one day early.

The 2025 cabin trip had begun as an ambitious double vacation: Ocean City followed by a peaceful retreat in the mountains. Instead, it became a chain reaction of bad decisions and infrastructure failures.

Maharko kissed a sick girl and brought a disease into the cabin. Leo developed kidney stones on the drive. The cabin had one bathroom and unreliable water. The living room reached ninety degrees. Half the group took mushrooms while already sick. Nick H psychologically tortured Nick F during his trip. Benji smoked cigarettes indoors. Lanternflies occupied the deck. Eric and Alex conducted nightly Ultraphunk attacks from behind a barricaded bedroom door.

By the time they left, nearly everyone was sick, sleep-deprived, or furious.

The cabin had won.
